Ghost REC WATCH
Live transcript
Ask anything, or type / for actions. Ghost only sees your screen or hears your mic when you turn on a capture icon.

Settings

Provider

Bring your own key. Keys are encrypted and stored only on this device — never sent anywhere but the provider you pick.

Models

Smart mode off uses the fast model; on uses the smart one. Leave blank for Ghost's default.

Custom / actions

Your own slash commands. Type /name in the input box to run one. The prompt tells Ghost what to do with your screen, transcript or question — for example Translate everything on screen to Spanish.

Appearance
Window

At 100% the panel is solid; lower it to see what's behind.

Screen sharing
Learn more

Ghost can try to hide its window from screen-share and recording apps on Windows using an OS-level capture-exclusion flag. This is best-effort only — it is not guaranteed against every screen-share, recording, or capture tool, and some meeting apps or OS updates may defeat it entirely. Only use this for legitimate purposes (private notes, personal assistance, accessibility) and always follow your organization's and your meeting counterparts' policies on recording and AI use.

Recording consent: the mic can pick up other people's voices, which are then sent to your AI provider. Many places require everyone to agree before a conversation is recorded or transcribed — ask first.

Shortcuts

Use names like CommandOrControl+Shift+G or Alt+F1. Leave blank to turn a shortcut off.
