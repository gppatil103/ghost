Screen sharing
Learn more

Ghost can try to hide its window from screen-share and recording apps on Windows using an OS-level capture-exclusion flag. This is best-effort only — it is not guaranteed against every screen-share, recording, or capture tool, and some meeting apps or OS updates may defeat it entirely. Only use this for legitimate purposes (private notes, personal assistance, accessibility) and always follow your organization's and your meeting counterparts' policies on recording and AI use.

Recording consent: Meeting capture sends other people's voices to your AI provider. Many places require everyone on a call to agree before it's recorded or transcribed — ask first.

Meeting audio
Learn more about Zoom and headsets

To let Ghost hear the other participants (not just you), Zoom needs to actually be playing their audio through your speakers. If you're on a headset, Ghost's "Meeting" capture uses Windows system-audio loopback, so muting your speakers or routing audio through an app-exclusive device will make meeting audio invisible to Ghost. Your own mic audio is captured separately and doesn't depend on this.
